03

Water reminder

Gentle altitude-based reminders while this page is open.

滴

Reminder every 45 minutes

The interval is 45 minutes below 2,500 m, 30 minutes from 2,500–3,999 m, and 20 minutes from 4,000 m. A reminder is only a prompt: a small drink of around 150–250 ml may suit many people; adjust to thirst and conditions, and do not force excessive water.

Planning reminder: 24 hours at this altitude.

Planning estimate: 12 hours below 2,500 m; 24 hours at 2,500 m, adding 24 hours for each additional 1,000 m. This is not an exact medical requirement or clearance to ascend. Acclimatization needs vary; follow expert/local guidance and never climb higher with altitude-illness symptoms.
